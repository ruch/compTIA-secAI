# compTIA-secAI
Prep for CompTIA SecAI+ exam
Tests created with Claude and Copilot

PracticeTest1 is an exhaustive question bank.
PracticeTest2 is an interactive test scores and explains the answers as you.


My experience passing the exam in two weeks and first attempt - 

- If you are in this space, a week or two may be enough with domain background
- The exam is 54 questions in 60 minutes, including unscored pilot questions you can not identify
- 64% of the exam is in two domains: AI security and securing AI — own these. Passing score is 67% so strength in these two is critical
- Flag Performance Based Questions at the start and return to them after completing the objective questions. This will take the time pressure off
- Use Claude and Copilot to generate practice exams from the official objectives PDF and exam questions
- Where multiple AI models agree on questions, those topics are canonical — study them hard
- The LinkedIn Learning course is a good foundation but needs to be supplemented
Know NIST, ISO, and the relevant legal/regulatory landscape
